Words suggest. Experiments test.

The primary experiment learns a constant and exponent by least-squares regression in log space, using only the fitting observations. A separate probe tests the frozen prediction. The optional language classifier learns six scaling meanings from 48 original synthetic English examples. It can misread unfamiliar wording, so you choose the meaning before anything is compared. It does not grade you.

The experiment uses a labelled mathematical relationship and its assumptions. The calculations are deterministic. No claim is made about real-world measurements or improved learning outcomes.

Model, evidence and limits

TF-IDF word and bigram features feed a trained six-class linear softmax classifier. English word normalization is declared in the source. The suggestion score is uncalibrated, not a probability of understanding.
